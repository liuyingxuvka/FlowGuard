## Why

FlowGuard has accumulated several independent review, search, template, and self-maintenance routes whose useful protections are now duplicated by the canonical DNA blueprint, topology, behavior commitments, affected-model propagation, ContractExhaustion, ModelMaturation, and current evidence owners. Keeping every historical behavior unchanged would preserve duplicate ownership, unnecessary prompts and model owners, and a public surface that makes the direct path harder to understand and maintain.

## What Changes

- Extend Architecture Reduction from behavior-preserving contraction only to an explicit, evidence-bound `retire_behavior` disposition for product behavior that is no longer necessary.
- **BREAKING** retire the independent Model Angle Deliberation and Maintenance Scan Router product routes after their remaining obligations move to TaskCoverageDemand, ModelMaturation, DevelopmentProcessFlow, and typed maintenance obligations.
- **BREAKING** reduce Model Similarity Consolidation to the small typed relations actually consumed by canonical DNA, affected topology, ContractExhaustion, CodeStructure, and ArchitectureReduction; remove its unused standalone planning/review route.
- Consolidate bug back-propagation into one ModelMiss → ContractExhaustion → ModelMaturation path, bounded by canonical DNA relations instead of repeated free-form analogous searches.
- Make risk-template search conditional on an explicit reuse need or a stable cross-project pattern instead of a universal model-completion gate.
- Remove the modeled-target lookup fallback in Existing Model Preflight: current modeled systems use exact DNA ownership; unmodeled systems enter an explicit adoption/candidate-discovery state that cannot claim current understanding.
- Remove release- or task-instance self models from current DNA after their still-required protections and code/test ownership move to continuing canonical owners. In this release, `readme_positioning_20260602`, `release_visibility_process`, `risk_purpose_header`, and `openspec_archive_cleanup` are historical execution models rather than continuing product owners.
- Remove five additional completed construction models from current DNA after exact negative-case transfer: `ai_surface_streamlining`, `reduce_architecture_surface`, `simplify_flowguard_structure`, `structure_surface_simplification`, and `simplify_field_schema` (the last merges into `default_replacement_field_lifecycle`). Keep immutable history, but do not execute these old rollout state machines as current product truth.
- Rewrite the three retained owners `validation_evidence_gates`, `user_facing_model_diagrams`, and `codex_skill_satellites` from one-time rollout checklists into permanent current-capability models bound to their real code, tests, specifications, prompt suite, and suite-map inputs.
- Repair the retained `minimum_valuable_model_entry` self model so it actually represents the new conditional template route instead of continuing to require the retired universal search/no-match/harvest sequence.
- Reduce retained routes internally as well as deleting whole routes: remove, merge, delegate, or make explicitly on-demand any branch, scan, reflection step, evidence projection, or payload construction that has no unique current obligation or repeats another exact owner at disproportionate token, time, memory, or validation cost.
- Consume the current per-model path-quality results owned by the coordinated `require-per-model-path-quality-closure` change when deciding which FlowGuard self-model and implementation contractions are evidence-ready; this retirement change does not create a second model optimizer.
- Make the existing Behavior Commitment Ledger source inventory mechanically current: resolve every direct, anchored, composite, and bounded-glob source reference, fingerprint the real files deterministically, and fail broad claims when the checked-in inventory is stale instead of trusting a handwritten `current` flag.
- Upgrade DevelopmentProcessFlow's existing conditional `strategy_selection` mode from caller-selected route validation to bounded order derivation: reject sequences that violate declared dependencies, calculate repeated writes, invalidated derived artifacts, repeated validation, coordination, and side-effect exposure from current step metadata, and select the least-cost route only within the declared finite equivalent candidate set.
- Restore the Behavior Commitment Ledger to its external-promise boundary: normative requirement, interface, user-visible workflow, and declared process-contract sources remain in BCL, while implementation modules, helper models, and tests bind through the software blueprint and implementation/test inventories instead of appearing as behavior-source authority.
- **BREAKING** remove redundant public templates, route registrations, top-level exports, dedicated self-model owners, and obsolete commitments for retired behavior; old names fail visibly and gain no alias, forwarder, compatibility reader, or fallback.
- Retain every core DNA safeguard: hierarchical input/state/output/effect/terminal contracts, code and test bindings, behavior commitments, affected topology, finite same-class cases, model-miss back-propagation, evidence freshness, and release/install ownership.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `architecture-reduction`: Add evidence-bound intentional behavior retirement and complete responsibility disposition.
- `model-angle-deliberation`: Retire the independent route and transfer its still-required coverage semantics to current owners.
- `maintenance-scan-router`: Retire the duplicate scan router while preserving typed maintenance obligations under current owners.
- `model-similarity-consolidation`: Retire the standalone consolidation engine while preserving the minimal typed relation handoff used by canonical owners.
- `model-miss-review`: Define one bounded bug-to-model-maturation path and remove repeated free-form analogous scanning.
- `contract-exhaustion-mesh`: Make canonical finite cases the only same-class defect authority consumed by Model Miss closure.
- `existing-model-preflight`: Replace modeled-target guessing and fallback with exact ownership or explicit non-authoritative adoption discovery.
- `flowguard-skill-kernel`: Make template search conditional and keep the compact kernel on the canonical DNA path.
- `minimum-valuable-model-entry`: Remove template search and harvest from the universal minimum model gate while retaining executable known-bad proof.
- `risk-template-library`: Trigger search and harvest only for explicit reuse work or evidence of a stable reusable cross-project pattern.
- `risk-evidence-ledger`: Consume one canonical same-class/model-maturation result instead of separate analogous-scan and model-angle gates.
- `flowguard-api-registry`: Remove retired routes, duplicate template commands, and internal helper re-exports from the public surface.
- `flowguard-route-topology-governance`: Remove retired route nodes and require every migrated protection to have one current owner.
- `authoritative-model-system`: Retire obsolete self-model owners and reject dangling model, code, test, behavior, or topology references after contraction.
- `flowguard-model-regression-orchestration`: Remove retired children from the current manifest only after replacement obligations and negative cases are bound to current owners.
- `behavior-commitment-ledger`: Compile and live-check the exact external-promise source inventory under the existing ledger owner, transfer implementation/test surfaces to their native blueprint inventories, and add no second scanner or caller-supplied freshness authority.
- `development-process-strategy-selection`: Derive and score candidate work orders from their dependency and invalidation graph instead of accepting any caller-preselected eligible candidate as preferred.
- `validation-evidence-gates`: Replace the old rollout checklist with the permanent evidence primitive, lifecycle, receipt, ownership, and terminal-claim contract.
- `user-facing-model-diagrams`: Make one retained owner cover deterministic Mermaid export plus optional, route-specific explanatory guidance without changing checker semantics.
- `flowguard-codex-skill-satellites`: Derive current suite topology from the canonical suite map and remove hard-coded historical counts, including stale “seven satellites” authority.

## Why

The v0.68.8 release established FlowGuard's current model authority and
portable-blueprint engine, but three user-visible gaps remain: the expensive
architecture-reduction protocol is admitted before it is needed, the
development-process skill names its internal modes without routing to their
detail contracts, and FlowGuard's own current portable DNA is not materialized
as one exchangeable artifact. The model/code/test evidence also needs an
explicit leaf-execution boundary so a green aggregate is not mistaken for
every leaf having run.

## What Changes

- Make high-cost ArchitectureReduction detail material explicitly on-demand
  and measure persistent prompt context separately from triggered detail.
- Route DevelopmentProcessFlow's plan-detailing and agent-workflow internal
  modes through exact conditional reference edges.
- Materialize one current FlowGuard self portable-blueprint artifact and verify
  it in isolation without source, provider, test, fallback, or reconstruction.
- Keep static, portable-integrity, and execution evidence separate, and make
  missing leaf execution evidence visible rather than manufacturing passes.
- Close the OpenSpec and release bookkeeping against one frozen v0.68.9
  source/model/install/Git/GitHub identity.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `prompt-budget`: persistent-context accounting and on-demand detail routing.
- `development-process-flow`: exact internal-mode reference routing.
- `portable-blueprint`: materialized self-DNA exchange and isolated integrity.
- `model-test-alignment`: explicit aggregate-versus-leaf execution boundary.

## Non-goals

- No independent reconstruction tool or routine reconstruction run.
- No language-specific FlowGuard core, compatibility reader, fallback path, or
  second current model authority.
- No global-optimum claim and no broad deletion without current equivalence
  proof.

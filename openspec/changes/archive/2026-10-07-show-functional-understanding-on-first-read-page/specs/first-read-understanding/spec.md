## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ordinary first-page understanding

A normal selected software-map read SHALL expose first-page current state, selected scope, target/gap denominators and the authentic task stopping basis. The existing compact task fixture SHALL make its complete summary and functional core visible on the first page without raising the page-size limit. The contract SHALL apply to general selected software maps, independently of a particular consumer product.

#### Scenario: A current task uses the default read

- **WHEN** an authenticated compact task with two actual owners and one requested outcome is read without read_batch
- **THEN** the first page exposes current and scope state, actual target and functional counts, original task IDs and stopping basis
- **AND** the read leaves Source and accepted authority unchanged

### Requirement: Original gap references and next owner

Navigation SHALL copy the actual original functional first gap, or the available original typed growth gap, including its full reason and next owner. It SHALL retain the actual report/detail raw reference when available. A missing file SHALL retain its missing-path observation with a null raw reference; no fingerprint or owner SHALL be guessed. Unavailable original gap detail SHALL remain unresolved. Original functional-scope, deepest-proven-layer and stopping decisions SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: An authentic blocked diagnostic is read

- **WHEN** the current task has an independently produced blocked diagnostic
- **THEN** first-page navigation preserves its original first gap, available raw reference, full reason, next owner and blocked disposition
- **AND** a caller-supplied closed claim cannot replace that decision

### Requirement: Complete selected denominators preserve unobserved facts

Denominators SHALL come from the complete selected material rather than a page slice. They SHALL retain selected IDs/count, distinct target count, separate observation/improvement/growth gap counts and separate unknown/scoped-out surface counts. Requested, satisfied, missing, required-obligation and required-owner counts SHALL be the distinct sizes of the corresponding original list or tuple; absent, null or unobserved collections SHALL produce null rather than zero. Transport SHALL preserve these null values and SHALL NOT claim complete decision basis from them.

#### Scenario: Wide material and missing task fields are transported

- **WHEN** complete selected targets, gaps and references span several pages, or original diagnostic/task material omits a task ID collection
- **THEN** each exposed denominator matches the independent full original material and an omitted collection remains null
- **AND** a paged first response remains detail-incomplete, omitted task facts keep decision_basis_complete false, and cursor reconstruction preserves every original record without duplication or omission

### Requirement: Typed compromises retain their obligations

Temporary-compromise counts and references SHALL derive only from authenticated typed temporary_compromise pointers. Their original affected outcomes, retained obligations and review conditions SHALL remain available through exact details. Ordinary suggestions SHALL NOT increase that count. Summary derivation SHALL NOT turn a compromise into a satisfied required outcome or alter the original task stopping disposition.

#### Scenario: A temporary compromise affects a required outcome

- **WHEN** a real typed compromise contains affected outcome, retained obligation and revisit-condition detail
- **THEN** its distinct count and original detail reference are readable, and the authentic missing outcome remains visible
- **AND** an ordinary suggestion is excluded from the compromise count

### Requirement: Unknown coverage stays separate from scoped-out coverage

The summary SHALL preserve the original independent inventory/binding distinction between required unbound surfaces and scoped-out surfaces. Unrequested live-file observation SHALL remain NOT_OBSERVED. Local selected evidence SHALL NOT be promoted to whole-software understanding or to a deeper functional proof layer.

#### Scenario: Selected inventory contains unknown and scoped-out surfaces

- **WHEN** independent selected inventory has one required unbound surface and one scoped-out surface and no live-growth observation was requested
- **THEN** unknown and scoped-out counts remain separate, NOT_OBSERVED is retained and the original deepest proven layer remains unchanged

### Requirement: Priority transport retains complete bounded lanes

Priority transport SHALL preserve all existing model, input, intent, relation, boundary, stale, architecture, summary and functional records and their exact references. Full detail lists SHALL travel once through the existing tags. Each serialized JSON page plus newline SHALL be at most 8192 UTF-8 bytes. All six cursor positions and accepted-head/scope binding SHALL remain checked. A complete explicit batch SHALL use one prepared projection and one final drift guard, with zero read-owned producers or authority writes. A terminal suffix page SHALL NOT claim complete selected details; a full batch SHALL claim completeness only after lossless reconstruction.

#### Scenario: A wide Unicode scope is paged and explicitly batched

- **WHEN** the selected closure spans pages and the caller separately requests a complete batch
- **THEN** all pages respect the byte limit, concatenated records match the independent full-payload oracle, and foreign-head, foreign-scope and modified cursors are rejected
- **AND** the terminal suffix remains detail-incomplete while the reconstructed full batch is complete under one preparation and one final guard

### Requirement: Indivisible evidence is never silently cut

An indivisible original evidence record exceeding the existing page bound SHALL produce the existing explicit blocked/error result. Transport SHALL NOT truncate a reason, remove an obligation or reference, expand the default page, or force a complete decision-basis claim to make that record fit.

#### Scenario: One original evidence record exceeds the page bound

- **WHEN** an original indivisible summary/evidence record exceeds 8192 UTF-8 bytes
- **THEN** transport reports the explicit size failure and preserves the complete original reason, obligations and references
- **AND** it does not claim complete decision basis for the failed response

### Requirement: Default protocol reads summary before complete closure

The consumer protocol SHALL start with first-page understanding, inspect selected current/target/gap/compromise/unknown denominators and original first-gap/next-owner, and follow current details or cursors when the decision basis is incomplete. Untransported detail SHALL NOT be treated as a proven empty set. read_batch:true SHALL remain an explicit request for tasks requiring the complete selected closure. The protocol SHALL introduce no recursive public read, arbitrary default cost cap, fallback reader or alternate evidence authority. Changed Source SHALL be qualified by existing native owners and current input identities; A-stage evidence SHALL NOT certify B-stage behavior.

#### Scenario: A normal caller follows the first-page protocol

- **WHEN** normal protocol guidance and a default read request are inspected
- **THEN** the guidance starts with first-page understanding and the request does not default to read_batch:true
- **AND** an explicit complete-closure request remains valid without any new cost or operation ceiling
